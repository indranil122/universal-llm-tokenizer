HOW IT ACTUALLY WORKS

Think of a tokenizer as an AI's reading glasses. Before an AI can understand a sentence, it breaks the words down into smaller chunks called 'tokens'. Sometimes a token is a whole word, sometimes it's just a syllable or a single letter. This tool lets you see exactly how different AI models slice up your text behind the scenes.
